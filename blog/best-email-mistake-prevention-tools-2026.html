Tool-by-Tool Breakdown

Smart Email Guard BEST VALUE

Free ($3.99/mo Pro)

Pre-send safety net that catches missing attachments, links, and CC recipients. 100% local — no data ever leaves your browser. Works on Gmail, Outlook, and Yahoo Mail. Built on Manifest V3.

Best for: Professionals who send important emails across multiple platforms and want zero-data-collection privacy.

Verdict: Best dedicated mistake-prevention tool. Only tool that covers all three mistake categories. Sideload install is the main friction point.

Gmail Built-in Tools

Free

Gmail's native attachment nudge (warns if you write "attached" but forgot the file) plus Undo Send (5-30 second recall window). Spelling and grammar check included.

Best for: Casual Gmail users who rarely make mistakes and don't need advanced protection.

Verdict: Good first line of defense, but inconsistent. Only catches basic attachment keywords. No link or CC checking.

Boomerang

$5-50/month

Email scheduling, follow-up reminders, and inbox pause. Millions of users. Not a mistake-prevention tool per se — its main value is scheduling and follow-up automation.

Best for: Sales teams and heavy schedulers who need send-later and follow-up reminders.

Verdict: Excellent for email workflow, but zero mistake-detection features. Complement, not replacement, for Smart Email Guard.

Mail Attach

Paid (commercial)

Dedicated Chrome extension for attachment reminders. Detects attachment keywords and warns before sending. Claims local processing.

Best for: Users who only care about attachment mistakes and don't need link/CC checks.

Verdict: Solid single-purpose tool. Narrower scope than Smart Email Guard (attachments only). Commercial pricing.

Grammarly

Free (Premium $12/mo)

AI-powered writing assistant with spelling, grammar, tone detection, and clarity suggestions. Browser extension and desktop app. 30M+ daily users.

Best for: Writers, non-native English speakers, and anyone who wants polished prose.

Verdict: Industry standard for writing quality. Complements mistake-prevention tools — Grammarly fixes the words, Smart Email Guard checks the logistics.

Superhuman

$30/month

Premium email client focused on speed. Keyboard shortcuts, AI triage, undo send. Gmail and Outlook only. Waitlist-based onboarding.

Best for: Power email users who spend 3+ hours/day in email and can justify $30/month for speed gains.

Verdict: Fastest email experience on the market. No dedicated mistake prevention — Undo Send is its only safety net. Premium price.

Start with Gmail's built-in nudge (free, no install). If it misses too many (which it will — see this HN thread), add Smart Email Guard for comprehensive detection across all three mistake types.

If you send sensitive/professional emails and need zero-data-collection

Smart Email Guard is the only option. Every other tool on this list processes your email on external servers. For client-confidential emails, legal communications, or financial documents, you cannot ethically use a server-side email checker.

Pair Grammarly (for writing quality) with Smart Email Guard (for logistics). They serve different purposes and work together seamlessly. Think of Grammarly as your editor and Smart Email Guard as your proofreader — one fixes the prose, the other catches the practical errors.
