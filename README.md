A free Chrome extension that catches missing attachments, links, and CC recipients before you hit Send — on Gmail, Outlook Web, and Yahoo Mail. No account. No cloud. No data collection. Everything runs locally in your browser.

## What it catches

- **Forgotten attachments** — You wrote "see attached" or "please find" but forgot the file
- **Missing links** — You referenced a URL in your email body but didn't include it
- **Empty CC field** — You mentioned "CC'ing Sarah" but Sarah isn't in the CC field

## How it works

Smart Email Guard scans your email compose window when you click Send (or hit Ctrl+Enter). If it finds trigger phrases without matching content, it shows a warning modal with two buttons: "Go Back" to fix it, or "Send Anyway" if you meant it.
